# Fly Brain Activities Used

FlyAlpha focuses on a small set of biologically motivated activities:

- sensory transduction into spikes;
- propagation through identified fly-like neurons and synaptic connectivity;
- sparse Kenyon-cell activity in the mushroom body;
- MBON output populations representing approach, avoidance, and hold behavior;
- PAM-like positive dopamine reinforcement;
- PPL1-like negative dopamine reinforcement;
- eligibility traces that remember recently active KC-to-MBON synapses;
- dopamine-gated KC-to-MBON plasticity.

The first scientific milestone is not profit. It is whether an initially
untrained fly-inspired circuit adapts in a controlled conditioning task and
loses that adaptation when the learned KC-to-MBON weights are reset.
