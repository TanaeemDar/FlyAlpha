# Modeling Assumptions

FlyAlpha is a research scaffold, not a claim that fruit flies evolved for
financial markets.

## Hard Constraints

- No conventional neural-network predictor.
- No transformers, boosted trees, policy networks, or backpropagation.
- Market features are deterministic transforms.
- Adaptive weights are limited to the mushroom-body-inspired KC-to-MBON layer.
- Dopamine signals modulate eligible synapses through a three-factor rule.

## Explicit Abstractions

- Leaky integrate-and-fire dynamics are a simulation choice, not per-neuron
  electrophysiology from MaleCNS.
- Structural synapse counts become executable weights through documented
  scaling.
- LONG, SHORT, and FLAT are deterministic interpretations of output population
  activity, not native fly behavior.
